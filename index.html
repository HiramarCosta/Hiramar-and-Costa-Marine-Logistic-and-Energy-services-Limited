About Us

Integrated solutions across maritime, oil and gas, and energy

Hiramar and Costa — Marine Logistics and Energy Services Limited is a Nigerian marine logistics and energy services company established to provide integrated solutions across the maritime, oil and gas, energy, logistics and offshore sectors.

What we are building toward

Our Vision

Recognised for operational excellence

To become a trusted marine logistics and energy services company recognised for operational excellence, safety, reliability and sustainable service delivery across Nigeria and international markets.
